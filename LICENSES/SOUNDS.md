# Sounds – sources & licenses (Haunted Town)

All sounds are real recordings released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synthesizer, no oscillators, no beeps. They were trimmed, faded, made mono and loudness-matched; looping beds got a crossfaded seam. `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 into `js/sounds-src.js` so the town also works via `file://`. The night-air reverb is a generated convolution impulse (processing only). Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily, by source ID only.
